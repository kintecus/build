# No docs on AdScale Confluence without team approval

## Decision

New documents and drafts must be approved by the internal Northwind team before being posted to AdScale's Confluence Hub pages.

## Context

The Phase 1 DoD draft was posted to the AdScale Confluence Hub page before internal alignment. ENG-LEAD and DELIVERY-LEAD pushed back on the quantified success metrics. DELIVERY-LEAD pulled the draft from the Hub page directly.

## Rule

1. Draft docs internally first (in pm-workspace or Google Docs)
2. Get approval from DELIVERY-LEAD and/or ENG-LEAD before posting to Confluence
3. This applies to all externally visible docs - specs, DoDs, status updates on AdScale's Confluence

Does not apply to Slack messages or sync agendas shared in the external channel.
