# Prompt: Critique My Idea

Read the project BRIEF.md and STATUS.md for context, then critique the idea I'm about to describe.

Structure your critique as:

1. **Strongest version** - Steel-man the idea first. What's the best case?
2. **Gaps and risks** - What am I missing? What could go wrong?
3. **Constraints check** - Does this fit within our time/resource/political constraints?
4. **Alternatives** - What other approaches achieve the same goal?
5. **Verdict** - If you were me, would you pursue this? Why or why not?

Be direct. I'm paying you to find problems, not to validate me.
